Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about Padel Analytics match tracking, health sync, subscriptions and privacy.

Does Padel Analytics detect strokes automatically?

No. You enter the score, sets, partner, opponents, effort and discomfort yourself right after playing. Automatic stroke or shot detection is not part of the current app.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Padel Analytics does not require a mandatory account to log and review your own matches.

Is my match data uploaded to a server?

No. Match data is kept on your device by default. There is no cloud database behind the app, and you decide if and when to export anything.

Can I connect Apple Health or Health Connect?

Yes. If you already recorded a padel session as a workout, Padel Analytics can import its duration, heart rate and energy expenditure so you do not have to re-enter it.

Can I log winners and errors, not just the score?

Yes, optionally. You can add a manual stroke tally with winners, forced and unforced errors, and overhead count. It is your own count, clearly kept separate from anything a sensor would measure.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Padel Analytics offers a trial period before a subscription is required, handled through Apple's or Google's standard in-app purchase system.

Is Padel Analytics a coaching platform?

No. It is a personal match and effort tracker for the athlete. There are no coach dashboards, team management or comment threads.

Which platforms are supported?

Padel Analytics targets iOS and Android as native apps. There is currently no Apple Watch app and no Garmin integration.
