FEATURES

Built For Serious Match Tracking

No guesswork and no invented numbers. Padel Analytics records exactly what you enter, plus the health data you choose to import.

01

Full Match Scoring

Record set and game scores, tiebreaks, golden point or advantage rules, and the final result of every match.

02

Partner & Rivals

Keep a history of who you played with and against, so patterns across pairings and opponents become visible over time.

03

Effort & Discomfort

Rate perceived effort and physical discomfort after each match to spot fatigue and overload before they become injuries.

04

Health Sync

Import duration, heart rate and energy expenditure from Apple Health or Health Connect for matches you already logged there.

05

Manual Stroke Tally

Optionally note winners, forced and unforced errors and overhead count by hand — reviewed data, never invented data.

06

Progress Over Time

See how your results, effort and discomfort evolve match after match, without spreadsheets or manual charts.
