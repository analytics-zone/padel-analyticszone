Privacy

How Padel Analytics handles match results, partner and opponent names, and health data with local-only storage and no mandatory account.

No account required

Your matches, your data

No mandatory account and no cloud upload. Match data stays on your device unless you choose to export it.

Privacy Policy

Padel Analytics is built around your own device. Match results, partner and opponent names, effort ratings and any health data you import stay on your device unless you choose to export them.

Last updated: July 2026

Local-only by default

  • No mandatory account to log and review your own matches.
  • No default cloud sync for match history, names, or health data.
  • No advertising trackers.
  • Export, when available, is something you choose to do.

Information you enter

Padel Analytics stores the match information you type in: set and game scores, your partner's name, your opponents' names, court side, dominant hand, perceived effort, discomfort level, notes, and an optional manual stroke tally. Partner and opponent names are personal information about other people that you choose to record; only enter names you are comfortable keeping on your own device.

Health data

If you grant permission, Padel Analytics can read matching padel sessions from Apple Health or Health Connect to import duration, heart rate and energy expenditure. This import happens on your device and is only used to complete your match record. Health data is treated with the same care as your other match data: it is not sold, and it is not used for advertising.

Subscription and payment

Subscriptions are handled by Apple's App Store or Google Play in-app purchase systems. Padel Analytics does not collect or store your payment card details; billing information is processed by the platform you purchased through.

Your control

You can edit or delete matches, partner and opponent names, and notes at any time from within the app. Contact us at analyticszone+padel@onmedic.org for privacy questions or deletion requests.
